## Notes on the correction

The hemistich at the center of the lesson, والقدر الله عرفني به في كنت ومقداره, is recited at 01:27, discussed at 07:42 through 07:54, and repeated at 08:04. The teacher connects it to the words حق قدره ومقداره العظيم in Salat al-Fatih, which is recited at 00:45. The surrounding explanation builds three images for qadr and miqdar: wealth measured in trillions that cannot be carried and stays in the bank, reachable only by check, the key without which no door and no hotel room opens, and the small creature in its burrow to which Allah sends its provision.

Both transcription passes produced stock hallucinations over the chanting and noise sections, such as اشتركوا في القناة and ترجمة نانسي قنقر, which are artifacts of the speech model's training on subtitled video and correspond to no speech in the recording. They were removed. The repeated "la, la, la" of the original transcription corresponds to the group recitation sections, whose exact litany wording is not recoverable word for word at this recording quality apart from the identifiable formulas noted above. Timestamps are accurate to within about one second. Passages marked uncertain preserve the most plausible reading of garbled audio and should be verified against the recording by a listener familiar with the speakers before quotation.
